LESSON 1

📐 Foundations of Technological Drawing

What is technological drawing?

Technological drawing is a visual language used to communicate the shape, size, structure, and specifications of an object or project accurately. It is used in fields such as engineering, architecture, manufacturing, electronics, and technical-vocational education.

📏 Lines

Different line types communicate different information. Object lines show visible edges; hidden lines show features that cannot be directly seen; center lines indicate centers or axes; dimension lines communicate measurements.

📊 Scale

Scale represents the relationship between the drawing size and the actual size. A drawing may be full-size, reduced, or enlarged depending on the purpose.

🔢 Dimensions

Dimensions communicate measurements such as length, width, height, diameter, and angles. They should be clear, accurate, and consistently placed.

👀 Views

Orthographic views commonly represent an object from different directions, such as front, top, and side views.

LESSON 2

🧰 Drawing Tools, Materials & Conventions

📏 T-square

Used to draw accurate horizontal lines and to guide triangles.

🔺 Triangles

Used with a T-square to create standard vertical and angled lines.

🧭 Compass

Used to draw circles and arcs with controlled radius.

✏️ Pencils

Technical pencils or lead grades are selected according to the required line quality and drafting task.

📐 Ruler/Scale

Used to measure and construct lines according to a required size or scale.

🧹 Eraser

Used to remove construction marks while keeping the drawing clean.

Good Drafting Practices

  • Keep tools clean and properly stored.
  • Use consistent line quality and accurate measurements.
  • Write labels and dimensions clearly.
  • Maintain a clean drawing sheet and organized layout.
  • Follow the required drawing standards and conventions.

LESSON 3

💻 ICT and Technological Drawing

Computer-Aided Design (CAD)

CAD refers to using computer software to create, modify, analyze, and document technical drawings or designs. Compared with manual drafting, CAD can make editing, duplication, measurement, and organization more efficient.

🖱️ Precision

Digital drawing tools can help users create accurate geometry, align objects, and enter exact dimensions.

🔄 Editing

Digital objects can be moved, copied, resized, or revised without redrawing the entire sheet.

🗂️ Organization

Layers, files, and drawing templates can help organize complex technical drawings.

📤 Output

Completed drawings can be saved, printed, exported, and shared in appropriate digital formats.
